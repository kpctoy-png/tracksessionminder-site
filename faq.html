Questions and answers

Sign-in, alerts, signal at the track, plans and more.

Which events use Track Session Minder?

Right now, Just Track It events. Other clubs can join after the App Store launch in early 2027.

Do I need to download anything?

Not today. Track Session Minder runs in your phone's web browser: scan the QR code at the track or open the link. On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app. iPhone and Android apps are coming in early 2027.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to view an event's schedule. To get your own schedule, alerts and coaching, use Find my name on the My Day tab: pick your name from the event's registered drivers and enter the last 4 digits of your cell number.

What does it cost?

Viewing an event's schedule needs no plan. Participant, which unlocks alerts, My Day, coaching and DTC, will be a yearly plan bought in the app that renews automatically. Clubs choose Practice, Qualify or Race. Pricing will be announced before the App Store launch.

Why am I not getting alerts on my iPhone?

On iPhone, alerts only work when the app is added to your Home Screen. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, open it from the new icon, and tap Turn on alerts on the Now tab.

I don't keep my phone on me at the track. Will I miss alerts?

Not if you wear a smartwatch. Leave your phone in your bag or car nearby and your Apple Watch, Wear OS watch or Galaxy Watch still alerts you to emergencies, session alerts, schedule changes and coaching or check ride reminders. The watch has to stay connected to your phone, so keep it within Bluetooth range (about 30 feet) or use a watch with Wi-Fi or cellular. If your phone is turned off, out of battery or far away with no connection, nothing reaches your watch.

Will alerts show on my smartwatch?

Yes. Alerts on your phone also show on a paired Apple Watch, Wear OS watch or Galaxy Watch, including session alerts, coaching and check ride reminders, and emergency announcements. There's no watch app to install. Turn on alerts on your phone, keep your watch's notifications on for your browser (or "Session Minder" on iPhone), and your watch buzzes when your phone is locked or you've left it nearby. Your watch needs to stay connected to your phone: within Bluetooth range (about 30 feet), or on Wi-Fi or its own cellular plan. Emergencies show as "🚨 Emergency" with a longer buzz. Never look at your watch or phone on track.

What if there's no signal at the track?

The app keeps the last schedule it loaded, so you can still see it. Anything you do while offline is sent as soon as you have signal again, and DTC answers from its saved FAQ until then.

Will it drain my battery?

No. Live updates pause while the app is in the background and catch up the moment you open it again.

Can DTC tell me how to drive a turn?

No. DTC never gives driving tips or instruction, and it never makes safety calls. The only exception is the club's own procedure for a spin, a breakdown or tow, or rain. Your instructor, the classroom and the drivers' meeting cover the rest.

Who can see my cell number?

Nobody in the app. The number comes from the club's registration, and the app only checks its last 4 digits to confirm it's you.

How do I book coaching?

Request it from My Day. The club confirms the booking and both you and your instructor get a reminder 15 minutes before.

Can my club use it?

Until the App Store launch in early 2027, Track Session Minder is exclusive to Just Track It events. After launch it's open to every club. Email tracksessionminder@gmail.com to get on the list, and we'll be in touch before launch.

Who makes Track Session Minder?

Redline Techniques LLC, a small South Carolina company. The app is built by Kevin Camire.
