How it works

No more crowding around a printed schedule or missing a session because the day ran late.

The club sets up the day

Organizers build the schedule in the app or import it from Google Sheets, Excel or a CSV file, with run groups, classes, meetings and lunch.

Drivers open the app

Scan the QR code at the track or tap the link. Viewing the schedule needs no account.

Changes reach everyone

Running late, a red flag, a lightning hold: one tap from the organizer and every phone updates at once.

Meet DTC, your Digital Track Companion

Where's the drivers' meeting? What's the procedure if I break down? DTC answers event questions from the club's own rules, website and notes, so organizers answer fewer of the same questions.

DTC never gives driving instruction or makes safety calls. Your instructor, the flags and the officials always come first.

Proven at the track

Track Session Minder ran its first live event with Just Track It at Roebling Road Raceway on October 31 to November 1, 2026, with drivers, instructors and organizers using it all weekend. Their feedback is shaping every update.
