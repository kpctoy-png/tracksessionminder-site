Phone not on you? Your watch has you covered.

Don't carry your phone around the paddock? Your Apple Watch, Wear OS or Galaxy Watch still alerts you to emergencies, grid calls, session alerts and schedule changes, and coaching and check ride reminders. No watch app needed.

Keep your phone nearby (in your bag or car) so your watch stays connected, or use a watch with Wi-Fi or cellular. Never look at your watch or phone on track.

Meet DTC, your Digital Track Companion

Where's the drivers' meeting? What's the procedure if I break down? DTC answers event questions from the club's own rules, website and notes, so organizers answer fewer of the same questions.

DTC never gives driving instruction or makes safety calls. Your instructor, the flags and the officials always come first.

Ready for a full paddock

  • All 203 registered drivers loaded from the club's registration list, and kept up to date through the weekend.
  • Drivers found their name and saw their own sessions, with grid calls on their phones and watches.
  • Every instructor saw their students' names, cars and sessions in the app.
  • Organizers had a printable pairings sheet that showed what changed with each registration update.
  • Track staff used their own view to look up cars by make, color or number.
