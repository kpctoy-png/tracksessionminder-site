Features

One app for everyone at the track: drivers, instructors and the people running the day.

For drivers

  • Now screen: what's on track, what's next, and a live countdown
  • Full schedule for each day, color-coded by run group
  • Alerts before your session and whenever the schedule changes
  • Emergency announcements like lightning or a red flag, to every phone
  • My Day: your run group, classes, meetings and coaching in one list
  • Find my name: sign in by picking your name and the last 4 digits of your cell
  • Works with weak signal and catches up when you're back in range
  • Easy on your battery: live updates pause while the app is in the background

For organizers

  • Simple Admin home: four big buttons for the jobs you do most
  • Change a session, run late or catch up, and every driver sees it
  • Announcements to everyone or chosen run groups, with an "Are you sure?" check
  • Import schedules and driver lists from Google Sheets, Excel or CSV files, or paste them straight in
  • Registered drivers brought in automatically for each event
  • Coaching requests and bookings, with clash checks for instructors
  • Incident reports recorded on the day
  • Driver feedback with star ratings after the event
  • Your club's logo on every event, and several admins per club

DTC, your Digital Track Companion

DTC answers event questions using the club's rules, website and notes: meeting times, tech, where to go, and the club's own procedures for a spin, a breakdown or rain.

  • Answers from your club's own information
  • Keeps working from a saved FAQ when signal drops
  • Never gives driving instruction or makes safety calls

Privacy built in

No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your information. Cell numbers are only used to confirm it's you, and the app never shows them.
