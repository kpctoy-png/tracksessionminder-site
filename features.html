Features

One app for drivers, instructors and organizers.

For drivers

  • Now screen: what's on track, what's next and a countdown

  • Grid call: an alert 10 minutes before your run group goes out, so you head to grid on time
  • Emergency announcements like lightning or a red flag
  • On your smartwatch too: alerts show on Apple Watch, Wear OS and Galaxy Watch
  • My Day: your run group, classes, meetings and coaching
  • Your instructor: if you're paired, see who you ride with on My Day and Now
  • Find my name: sign in with your name and cell number
  • No cell on file? Sign in with your email instead
  • Finished sessions fold away so the day stays easy to read
  • Works with weak signal and catches up when you're back
  • Easy on your battery: updates pause in the background

Alerts on your wrist

Don't keep your phone on you at the track? Your smartwatch still tells you about emergencies, session alerts, schedule changes and reminders while you're away from your phone, as long as it's still connected to it.

Turn on alerts once on your phone and they show up on your paired smartwatch. No watch app to install.

  • Works with Apple Watch, Wear OS and Galaxy Watch
  • Grid calls, session alerts and schedule changes
  • Coaching and check ride reminders 15 minutes before
  • Emergencies stand out: "🚨 Emergency" title and a longer buzz

Your watch needs to stay connected to your phone: within Bluetooth range (about 30 feet), or on Wi-Fi or its own cellular plan if your watch has one. Phone in your bag or car nearby? You're covered. Never look at your watch or phone on track. Watch the flags.

For organizers

  • Simple Admin home with four big buttons for daily jobs
  • Change a session, run late or catch up in one tap
  • Announcements to everyone or chosen run groups
  • Import from Google Sheets, Excel, CSV or a paste
  • Registered drivers brought in automatically for each event
  • Instructor pairings come in with them and show on each driver
  • See who's signed in with a ✓, or filter to "Not signed in yet"

  • Driver feedback with star ratings after the event
  • Your club's logo on every event, with several admins

DTC, your Digital Track Companion

DTC answers event questions using the club's rules, website and notes: meeting times, tech, where to go, and the club's own procedures for a spin, a breakdown or rain.

  • Answers from your club's own information
  • Keeps answering from a saved FAQ when signal drops
  • Never gives driving tips or makes safety calls

Privacy built in

No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your information. Cell numbers are only used to confirm it's you, and the app never shows them.
