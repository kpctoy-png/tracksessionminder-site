About Track Session Minder

From the first briefing to the last checkered flag, Track Session Minder makes track days safer and smoother, with one app connecting organizers, instructors and drivers.

Why it exists

A track day runs on a tight schedule, and that schedule changes all day: an incident, a long lunch, a storm rolling in. When the word only travels by printed sheet and shouting across the paddock, drivers miss sessions and organizers spend the day repeating themselves.

Track Session Minder puts the live schedule on every phone, so one change from the organizer reaches every driver and instructor at once.

What guides every feature

  • Safer: urgent messages reach every driver fast
  • Smoother: less waiting, fewer missed sessions
  • Connected: organizers, instructors and drivers on one app

Who makes it

Track Session Minder is built by Kevin Camire and made by Redline Techniques LLC, a small company in South Carolina. Its first live event was with Just Track It at Roebling Road Raceway on October 31 to November 1, 2026.

The colors come from one of motorsport's most famous liveries: Gulf light blue and orange.
