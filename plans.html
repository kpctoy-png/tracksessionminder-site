What clubs can do, with examples

PreliminaryThese are features clubs use today in testing with Just Track It. They may change, move between plans, or be added before launch.
📅

Live schedule changes

Change a session, run late or catch up in one tap, and every driver's phone updates at once.

Example: Lunch runs long. Tap +10 min and every session after lunch moves. Drivers get “Schedule moved 10 min later” on their phone and watch, and their grid calls move with it.

📣

Announcements to the right people

Send to every run group or only the ones that need it.

Example: “Advanced group: hot pits closed for the next session” goes only to Advanced. Novice drivers in the classroom aren't buzzed.

🚨

Emergency announcements

A red banner across every screen, with a longer buzz on phones and watches. Even spectators viewing the schedule get it.

Example: “Track is closed. All cars to the paddock.” It backs up your flaggers and radio. It never replaces them.

🗂️

Registered drivers, pulled for you

Confirmed registrations come in from your registration site two days before, the day before and each event morning.

Example: The morning of the event, the list already matches your registration site. Only confirmed entries come in, never wait-listed or cancelled ones.

✏️

Registration-day changes

Fix things at registration without breaking the list.

Example: A driver shows up in a different car. Tap their name, change the car and number, or move them to another run group. The change survives the next morning's pull, and + Walk-in adds a driver who arrives without registering online.

✅

See who's signed in

Drivers sign in with Find my name: their name plus the last 4 digits of their cell.

Example: Admin shows 148 of 190 signed in. Filter to “Not signed in yet” and remind them at the drivers' meeting.

🤝

Instructor pairings and grid calls

Pair students with instructors, and both are told when to head to grid.

Example: Your instructor sees each student's name, car number and car, with Text, Call and Email buttons. Ten minutes before the session, both get “Head to grid now.”

🗓️

Coaching bookings with clash warnings

Drivers can request coaching from My Day, and your club confirms the booking.

Example: You book a driver with an instructor at 10:20, but that instructor is already booked or on track then. The app warns you before you save. Both get a reminder 15 minutes before.

🦺

Event staff mode

Read-only access for track staff with your club's staff code. No sign-in needed. You turn it on for each event.

Example: A tech or grid worker searches “red Miata”, filters to “on track now”, and finds the car number and run group in seconds.

📝

Incident reports

Record what happened on the day while it's fresh.

Example: Log an off with the car number, session and notes, send the driver a copy, and export everything to a spreadsheet after the event.

💬

DTC trained on your club

Your Digital Track Companion answers event questions from your rules, website and notes.

Example: “When is the novice meeting?” “Where is tech?” DTC answers from your notes and repeats your own spin, breakdown and rain procedures exactly as written. It never gives driving tips.

📥

Easy import and feedback

Bring in your schedule template and hear from drivers afterward.

Example: Paste a Google Sheets link to your daily schedule and the day is built. After the event, star ratings and comments come in, and you mark each one Done.
